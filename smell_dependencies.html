Formulas

PD — General (abstract)

\[ PD_i = w \cdot \text{severity}(s_i) \cdot \text{freq}(s_i) + \sum\text{pos\_out}(s_i) - \sum\text{neg\_out}(s_i) \]
  • \(w = 0.5\) — weight constant
  • \(\text{freq}\) — occurrence count (future work)
  • severity: HIGH=3, MED=2, LOW=1

PD — Concrete (implementation)

\[ PD^{\text{conc}}_i = w \cdot \text{severity}(s_i) + \sum\text{pos\_out}^{\text{conc}}(s_i) - \sum\text{neg\_out}^{\text{abs}}(s_i) \]
  • pos_outconc: co-located smells found in actual source file
  • neg_outabs: catalogue rules (Markovič & Polášek)

State heuristic h(S)

\[ h(S) = \sum_{s \in S} \text{severity}(s) \]
  • Total remaining severity of all active smells
  • Goal state: \(h(S) = 0\)

State transition

\[ S' = (S \setminus \text{resolved}(s_i)) \cup \text{created}(s_i) \]
  • resolved: smells eliminated by refactoring \(r(s_i)\)
  • created: new smells from negative dependencies

Greedy selection

\[ r^* = \arg\max_{s_i \in S}\; PD_i \]
  • Picks highest PD at each step — depth-1 lookahead only
  • Ignores cascading neg-deps

PD extended (cross-file scope, proposed)

\[ PD'_i = PD_i + \delta \cdot \text{files\_affected}(s_i) \]
  • \(\delta\) — cross-file scope weight
  • Accounts for smells spanning multiple files
